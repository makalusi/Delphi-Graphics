Frequently Asked Questions

We accept PDF, AI (Adobe Illustrator), EPS, and high-resolution PNG or JPEG files at 300 DPI or higher. PDF is preferred for print-ready files. If you're unsure about your file, send it to us and we'll check it for you before production.
Most standard orders are ready within 24–48 hours after artwork approval and payment confirmation. Larger or more complex jobs (e.g. vehicle branding, bulk apparel) may take 3–5 business days. Rush same-day options are available for select products — contact us to confirm availability.
Minimum quantities vary by product. Most printing services (flyers, business cards, stickers) start from as low as 50 pieces. Apparel branding typically starts from 10 pieces. We cater to both small businesses and large corporate orders — reach out and we'll work with your requirements.
We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and cash payments. A deposit or full payment is required before production begins. For large corporate orders, we can discuss invoicing arrangements. Contact us for payment details after receiving your quote.
Absolutely. Our in-house design team can create artwork from scratch — logos, layouts, branding materials, and print-ready files. Design is quoted separately and a proof will be sent for your approval before any printing begins. Just describe your vision and we'll bring it to life.
You're welcome to collect from our office at Sunrays House, 1st Floor, Room 108, Nairobi. We also offer delivery within Nairobi at an additional cost depending on location and order size. For upcountry orders, we arrange courier delivery — contact us to discuss logistics and costs before placing your order.
